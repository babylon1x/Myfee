# Business Brief: MyFee

## The business
MyFee is a school fees portal for high-end private schools in Abuja. Schools pay; parents use it free.

The school adds every pupil and links each one to a parent, who joins by email invite only. Parents see each child's fees for the term, what is paid and what is still owed, and pay each fee in full through Paystack, or directly to the school.

## The people who use it
- **Parents and guardians**, aged 28 to 55, often with several children in the school. Mostly on phones, with varying connections. Comfortable paying online, but not technical. They will not read instructions. Each parent has their own login; a second parent can be linked to the same children.
- **School staff** (bursar or admissions officer), on laptops, who admit pupils, send invites and record offline payments.
- **The school owner**, who sets the fees for each class and manages staff.

## The one thing that must work
A parent opens a child's tab and sees the correct fees for that child this term: what is paid, what is still owed, and when it is due. A fee shows as paid only once Paystack confirms it or the bursar approves a recorded offline payment; a receipt is then created.

## Three questions parents ask repeatedly
1. "How much do I still owe for this child this term?"
2. "When is the deadline, and what happens if I pay late?"
3. "What is this fee for, and do I have to pay it?"

Only the school can answer these. MyFee's assistant answers only from this school's documents and this parent's own children's records.

## Scope
Fees are set per class per term, as required (tuition, books, exams) or optional (excursions, clubs). Full payment only. No part payments, results, attendance or report cards yet.

## How the interface should feel, and why
Parents pay large fees and expect banking-app polish. A balance on screen can still feel like being chased for money. MyFee must feel **clear, calm and quietly premium**, never like a debt reminder.

- **Numbers are easy to read.** Amounts and due dates stand out.
- **Refined but steady fonts.** Warm enough for a school, serious enough for money.
- **Works on any phone, on slow data**, with few images.
- **Intentional colour.** Red is kept for real errors, not money owed.
